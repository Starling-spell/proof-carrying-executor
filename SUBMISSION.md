# Portal draft

ProofCarryingExecutor is a reusable GenLayer execution-authorization primitive for autonomous agents. A policy owner and assigned agent establish an epoch anchored to independently fetched intent and manifest fingerprints. Every attempt binds a logical action, retry number, predecessor, parent head and canonical action hash. Validators refetch both baselines and the action, independently reconstruct the complete bounded decision packet, and require exact agreement. Intent mutation yields BASELINE_DRIFT; identity mutation yields IDENTITY_BREAK; unavailable or unsafe attempts remain immutable and do not advance the logical head. Only the latest AUTHORIZED attempt under the active epoch can be consumed. Consumption is one-time, dispatches only to an allowlisted adapter, charges budget, advances the head and stores an exact execution receipt. The demo adapter independently rejects replayed authorization IDs.
